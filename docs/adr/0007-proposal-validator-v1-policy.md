# v1 proposal validator policy

Study OS locks a **mechanical** validator for **validated proposals**: hard-reject illegal targets (unknown teaching-profile dim; mastery KC not in pack `mastery.yaml`; mastery proposals when `mastery.yaml` is missing/empty), missing/malformed **evidence briefs**, and absolute values outside **0.0–1.0**. **Decay** means shrink |\Δ| to a max step of **0.15** (then **clamp** into [0, 1]) — not between-session score fading; a decayed apply counts as **accept**. Absolute exceptions: **first mastery write** while the KC score is absent, and **bootstrap** teaching-profile seeding only — thereafter all updates use ±0.15 + decay (default 0.5 is not a free absolute reset). At most **3 accepts** per tutor loop; same-target accepts are **last-wins** in order. Evidence briefs are a **presence/schema gate only** (contents do not grade the proposal). **Rejects** persist the proposal record + brief without applying a delta. Rejected alternatives: judgmental/LLM evidence scoring, soft kind-allowlists for accept, merge/average of conflicting proposals, and automatic inter-session mastery/profile decay.
